Internship Cover Letter Template

Built for students and freshers who want to write a clean, professional, and internship-focused cover letter. Choose a version, personalize quickly, and send with confidence.

How to use this template

  1. Select the format: General Internship or Role-Specific Internship.
  2. Update applicant, recipient, role, and company details first.
  3. Keep the body focused on relevant skills and project outcomes.
  4. Copy the final letter to your document or email editor.
  5. Proofread before sending and personalize for each company.

Cover Letter Tips

  • Personalize the company name and role in every draft.
  • Mention only skills and projects relevant to the internship.
  • Keep it short, direct, and easy to scan.
  • Avoid using the exact same letter for every application.
