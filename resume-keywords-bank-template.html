Resume Keywords Bank

A practical ATS-friendly keyword bank for freshers and students. Use role-relevant terms naturally to make your resume stronger and easier for recruiters to scan.

How to use this template

  1. Choose categories that match your target role and job description.
  2. Pick only keywords that honestly reflect your skills and projects.
  3. Add selected keywords in summary, skills, projects, and experience sections.
  4. Copy individual categories or copy the full bank for quick editing.
  5. Review final resume language for clarity, relevance, and readability.

Resume Keyword Tips

  • Use keywords naturally in sentences.
  • Match terms with the job description.
  • Do not overstuff your resume.
  • Keep keywords relevant to your profile.
