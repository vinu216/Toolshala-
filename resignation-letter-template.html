Resignation Letter Template

A formal, ready-to-edit resignation letter format for professionals and interns. Choose a version, personalize key details, and submit a respectful resignation in minutes.

How to use this template

  1. Select the right format: Formal, Short, or Internship resignation letter.
  2. Update sender, recipient, subject, and last working date first.
  3. Keep your tone polite, clear, and professional.
  4. Copy the final resignation letter to your email or printed document.
  5. Proofread names, dates, and designation before submitting.

Resignation Letter Tips

  • Be polite and concise in your wording.
  • Mention your last working day clearly.
  • Thank the company and team sincerely.
  • Avoid negative comments or complaints.

Tip: edit any section directly, then copy, download as text, or print your formal resignation letter.
