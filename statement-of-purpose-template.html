Statement of Purpose (SOP) Template

A structured SOP format for university admissions, scholarships, and academic programs. Choose a style, personalize each section, and keep your story clear and focused.

How to use this template

  1. Select a style: Academic SOP or Scholarship SOP.
  2. Edit each section with specific, role-relevant details.
  3. Keep your story connected from background to goals.
  4. Copy the final SOP and paste into your application portal or document.
  5. Customize for each university, program, or scholarship.

SOP Writing Tips

  • Be specific and avoid vague statements.
  • Tell a clear story from motivation to future goals.
  • Mention relevant projects, research, or achievements.
  • Customize each SOP for the target university/program.
