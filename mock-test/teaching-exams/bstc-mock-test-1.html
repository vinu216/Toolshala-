Rajasthan BSTC Preparation

BSTC Mock Test 1

This Pre D.El.Ed mock test follows the latest exam-oriented pattern with balanced easy-to-moderate questions across Mental Ability, Rajasthan GK, Teaching Aptitude, Hindi, and English.

Total Questions: 40

Duration: 45 Minutes

Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Type: Section-wise MCQ

Instructions

  • Each question has one correct answer.
  • Attempt all sections in sequence to simulate real exam flow.
  • Mark doubtful questions and review in final 5 minutes.
  • Use the answer key at the end for self-evaluation.

Section A: Mental Ability / Reasoning (Q1–Q8)

  1. Find the next number: 3, 6, 12, 24, ?
    A) 30 B) 36 C) 48 D) 54
  2. Book : Read :: Food : ?
    A) Sleep B) Eat C) Cook D) Serve
  3. If CAT is coded as DBU, then DOG is coded as:
    A) CPH B) EPH C) FQI D) EOG
  4. Ravi walks 5 km north, then 3 km east. In which direction is he from the start point?
    A) North-West B) South-East C) North-East D) South-West
  5. Point out the blood relation: A is the brother of B. B is the mother of C. A is C's:
    A) Father B) Uncle C) Brother D) Grandfather
  6. Find the odd one out: Triangle, Square, Circle, Cube
    A) Triangle B) Square C) Circle D) Cube
  7. In a row of students, Rina is 8th from left and 12th from right. Total students are:
    A) 18 B) 19 C) 20 D) 21
  8. Choose the pair that is similar: Pen : Write :: Knife : ?
    A) Cut B) Draw C) Hold D) Carry

Section B: Rajasthan General Knowledge (Q9–Q16)

  1. Which city is known as the Pink City?
    A) Udaipur B) Jaipur C) Jodhpur D) Kota
  2. Ghoomar is a famous:
    A) Folk dance B) Fort C) River D) Festival
  3. Keoladeo National Park is located in:
    A) Bharatpur B) Ajmer C) Churu D) Bikaner
  4. The largest district of Rajasthan by area is:
    A) Jaisalmer B) Barmer C) Bikaner D) Udaipur
  5. Which lake is famous in Udaipur?
    A) Sambhar Lake B) Ana Sagar C) Pichola Lake D) Kolayat Lake
  6. Who built Hawa Mahal?
    A) Sawai Jai Singh II B) Pratap Singh C) Man Singh I D) Rana Kumbha
  7. Desert National Park is primarily in:
    A) Alwar B) Jaisalmer C) Kota D) Jhunjhunu
  8. Gangaur festival is mainly associated with worship of:
    A) Lord Krishna B) Lord Shiva and Goddess Gauri C) Hanuman D) Surya

Section C: Teaching Aptitude (Q17–Q24)

  1. Child-centered education mainly focuses on:
    A) Teacher's lecture B) Syllabus completion only C) Learner needs and participation D) Punishment
  2. The best way to motivate primary students is:
    A) Frequent scolding B) Only homework C) Positive reinforcement D) Ignoring weak learners
  3. Formative assessment is used for:
    A) Final ranking only B) Continuous improvement C) Punishment D) Attendance
  4. Inclusive classroom means:
    A) Teaching only toppers B) Same method for all C) Equal opportunities for diverse learners D) Excluding CWSN
  5. A good teacher quality is:
    A) Rigid behavior B) Bias in class C) Patience and empathy D) Avoiding questions
  6. When students are distracted, a teacher should first:
    A) Stop class permanently B) Use engaging activity C) Send all out D) Complain only
  7. Which method improves learning retention most in young learners?
    A) Passive listening B) Rote memorization only C) Activity-based learning D) Long dictation
  8. Diagnostic test is mainly conducted to:
    A) Identify learning gaps B) Award prizes C) Compare schools D) Increase syllabus

Section D: Hindi (Q25–Q32)

  1. 'अंधकार' का विलोम शब्द है:
    A) प्रकाश B) छाया C) रात D) संध्या
  2. 'जल्दी' का पर्यायवाची चुनिए:
    A) मंद B) शीघ्र C) विलंब D) रुकावट
  3. 'राम और श्याम बाजार गए।' यह किस प्रकार का वाक्य है?
    A) सरल B) संयुक्त C) मिश्र D) विस्मयादिबोधक
  4. शुद्ध वाक्य चुनिए:
    A) मुझे भूख लगा है B) मुझे भूख लगी है C) मुझे भूख लगे है D) मुझे भूख लगी हैं
  5. 'राजपुत्र' में कौन-सा समास है?
    A) तत्पुरुष B) द्वंद्व C) कर्मधारय D) बहुव्रीहि
  6. 'नदी' शब्द का बहुवचन है:
    A) नदीयों B) नदियाँ C) नदियॉ D) नदिया
  7. 'नाक कटना' मुहावरे का अर्थ है:
    A) घायल होना B) अपमान होना C) क्रोधित होना D) थक जाना
  8. 'दिन-रात एक करना' लोकोक्ति/मुहावरे का अर्थ है:
    A) सोना B) परिश्रम करना C) घूमना D) हार मानना

Section E: English (Q33–Q40)

  1. Choose the correct article: She is ___ honest teacher.
    A) a B) an C) the D) no article
  2. Choose the correct preposition: The book is ___ the table.
    A) in B) on C) at D) for
  3. Choose the correct tense: They ___ to school every day.
    A) goes B) go C) gone D) going
  4. Choose the correct form: Neither of the boys ___ present.
    A) are B) were C) is D) have
  5. Synonym of 'brave' is:
    A) coward B) bold C) weak D) dull
  6. Antonym of 'ancient' is:
    A) old B) antique C) modern D) historic
  7. Find the correctly written sentence:
    A) He don't like milk. B) He doesn't likes milk. C) He doesn't like milk. D) He not like milk.
  8. Choose the correct word: We should ___ our teachers.
    A) respects B) respected C) respect D) respecting
