Remote Internship Track for Indian Students

Internship

Eligibility: College Students / Freshers

Deadline: Rolling openings

Mode: Remote

Overview

This track helps Indian students and freshers prepare for remote internships across tech, marketing, design, operations, content, and business support. Use it to shortlist roles, prepare proof-of-work, and apply with stronger context instead of mass-applying.

What You'll Gain

  • Practical experience with real projects, tasks, and team workflows
  • Portfolio proof through samples, case studies, dashboards, or shipped work
  • Better clarity on remote work expectations and communication habits
  • Flexible remote, part-time, or hybrid internship options

Who Should Apply

This track is suitable for college students, freshers, self-taught learners, and early-stage creators who can show basic skills through coursework, projects, samples, or tool-based work.

How to Apply

  1. Pick one domain and review the eligibility, mode, and deadline details carefully
  2. Prepare your updated resume, portfolio links, and 2–3 relevant samples
  3. Write a short, clear application message customized to the role
  4. Apply through verified official sources and track follow-ups weekly

Important Note

ToolShala shares this opportunity for informational purposes. Please verify all details, deadlines, and requirements from the official source before applying.
