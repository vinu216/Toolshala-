Weekly Study Planner

Organize your week with clear study time blocks, subject priorities, revision sessions, and break slots. Pick a simple or detailed version, edit quickly, and keep your study routine realistic and consistent.

How to use this template

  1. Choose the planner mode: Simple Weekly Plan or Detailed Weekly Plan.
  2. Fill in goals for each day and assign subjects per time block.
  3. Keep one fixed revision block daily, especially for weak subjects.
  4. Add short breaks to stay focused without burning out.
  5. Review your week every Sunday and adjust next week accordingly.

Study Planning Tips

  • Don’t overload your schedule—focus on realistic daily targets.
  • Keep breaks short and intentional to protect momentum.
  • Revise weak subjects more often than strong subjects.
  • Review the plan weekly and improve it based on actual progress.
