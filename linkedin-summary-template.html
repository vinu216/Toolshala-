LinkedIn Summary Template

Write a profile summary that sounds clear, human, and confident. Pick a version for Student, Fresher, or Freelancer, customize quickly, and use it in your LinkedIn About section.

How to use this template

  1. Select the summary version that matches your current profile stage.
  2. Edit each section with your real skills, projects, and goals.
  3. Keep sentences short and easy to scan on mobile.
  4. Copy your final summary and paste it into LinkedIn About.
  5. Update your summary every few months as your profile grows.

LinkedIn Summary Tips

  • Keep it authentic—write in your own voice.
  • Mention your current goal clearly.
  • Add relevant keywords for your target roles.
  • Keep it easy to read with short lines.
