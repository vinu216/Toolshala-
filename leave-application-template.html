Leave Application Template

A formal, ready-to-edit leave application format for school, college, and office use. Choose a version, update the details, and submit a respectful request in minutes.

How to use this template

  1. Select the correct format: School, College, or Office leave application.
  2. Update sender, recipient, subject, and leave dates first.
  3. Keep your reason short, clear, and genuine.
  4. Copy the final application to your email or document file.
  5. Proofread names and dates before submitting.

Leave Application Tips

  • Keep the reason short and genuine.
  • Mention exact start and end dates.
  • Use a polite and respectful tone.
  • Proofread before submitting.
