Salary Negotiation Email Template

Negotiate your compensation respectfully after receiving an offer. Choose a soft or confident version, personalize the email, and keep your message clear, professional, and value-focused.

How to use this template

  1. Select the negotiation style that fits your situation.
  2. Personalize your role details, offer amount, and expected range.
  3. Mention clear value with skills, projects, or outcomes.
  4. Keep the ask professional and open to discussion.
  5. Proofread before sending to avoid tone mistakes.

Negotiation Tips

  • Be polite and professional.
  • Mention your value clearly.
  • Don’t sound entitled.
  • Keep it concise and specific.
