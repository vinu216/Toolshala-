Professional Email Format

A ready-to-use format for students and freshers writing internship applications, job outreach emails, follow-ups, and formal requests. Keep your email clear, polite, and action-focused.

How to use this template

  1. Select an example format based on your situation.
  2. Customize subject, recipient name, role, and company details.
  3. Keep paragraphs short and highlight your purpose clearly.
  4. Copy the final email and paste into your email client.
  5. Proofread once before sending.

Quick Email Tips

  • Keep subject lines short and specific.
  • Always mention your purpose clearly in the first lines.
  • Use polite, direct language and avoid slang.
  • Proofread grammar, names, and links before sending.
